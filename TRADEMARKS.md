# Name and logo

"Vernier", the wordmark with the amber I, the icon and the slogan are the identity of this project. **They are not covered by the AGPL
or the CERN-OHL-S**, which license the code and the designs, not the name. The files in `assets/brand/` are all rights reserved, with one
exception: you may use them unchanged to refer to this project (for example a link, a news item or a review).

## What you may do

- Fork, modify and redistribute the code and the designs under their licences.
- Say that your work is based on, compatible with or derived from this project.

## What you may not do

- Call your fork, your product or your service "Vernier", or use the logo, so that it can be taken for this project or for something that
  has its approval.
- Use the name or the logo on hardware or services that you sell without written permission.

## Status of the name

"Vernier" is a **working name**. No trademark has been registered and the search for earlier marks has not been done. A company called
Vernier Software & Technology sells laboratory sensors and software under a similar name, so the name may have to change (the fallback
is "Nonius"). If it does, this file and `assets/brand/` change with it.
