# Contributor grant

**Draft: have a lawyer review it before the first outside contribution is accepted.**

A project that sells a commercial licence can only do it with code whose authors allowed it. So that your contribution can be part of
that, every contribution needs this grant.

By sending a contribution (a pull request, a patch or any other file) you state and agree that:

1. **It is yours to give**: you wrote it, or you have the right to submit it under these terms (your employer included).
2. **Open licences**: it is licensed to everyone under the licence of the folder it goes into: the AGPL-3.0 for `firmware/`, `host/`,
   `tools/` and `simulation/`, and the CERN-OHL-S v2 for `cad/` and `docs/`.
3. **Extra grant**: you also grant the copyright holder (AIAYN-creator, and whoever it transfers the project to) a perpetual, worldwide,
   non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use, modify, sublicense and relicense your contribution **under any terms,
   including commercial licences and licences that are not open**.
4. **You keep your copyright**: this grant does not transfer it, and you may use your contribution as you wish.
5. **No guarantee**: the contribution is provided as it is, with no warranty.
6. **Sign-off**: you add `Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email>` to every commit, which means you accept this grant (the Developer
   Certificate of Origin, https://developercertificate.org, plus point 3 above).

Safety-relevant changes (emergency stop, limits, watchdog, anything that can move hardware) also follow the rules in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
